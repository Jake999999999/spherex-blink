01 · The Challenge & Solution 02 / 07

Making 102-Band All-Sky Infrared Data Effortless

SPHEREx maps the entire sky every six months across 6 detectors and 102 wavelengths—an extraordinary dataset locked behind heavy FITS pipelines.

The Barrier Today

  • Raw FITS friction: Exploring multi-epoch spectral exposures requires downloading gigabytes of files and writing custom Astropy scripts.
  • Hard to spot subtle change: Proper motion across 6-month SPHEREx visits is only 1–2 pixels, easy to miss without historical context.
  • Colour fakes variability: Subtracting exposures taken at different wavelengths leaves 10–15% false residuals around every normal star.

How SPHEREx Blink Solves It

  • Instant browser streaming: Fetches only the needed sky rows from NASA's public AWS S3 mirror—or runs with a local Python server for full archive queries.
  • 16-year blink timeline: Pairs 2010 AllWISE and 2014–2024 NEOWISE frames with 2025–2026 SPHEREx visits so slow stars visibly glide.
  • Wavelength-matched subtraction: Compares visits at matching wavelengths first, cancelling steady stars cleanly so movers & variables pop out.

Featured Targets Included Out of the Box

Each built-in target demonstrates a distinct astrophysical signature using real SPHEREx, WISE, NEOWISE, and Gaia DR3 data.

Barnard's Star

HIGH PROPER MOTION · 10.3″/yr

Crosses nearly 3 arcminutes between 2010 WISE and 2026 SPHEREx. Blinking turns its motion into an unmistakable glide across the field.

Epsilon Indi B

METHANE BROWN DWARFS · T1 / T6

Methane (CH₄) and water (H₂O) swallow green light in the Methane preset, making the brown dwarfs glow pink while normal stars stay blue-white.

WISE 0855−0714

COLDEST BROWN DWARF · ~250 K

Invisible at short near-IR bands (D1–D4), then emerges clearly at 4–5 µm (D6) as thermal emission peaks in the mid-infrared.

R Draconis & NEP-LPV

Long-period pulsating giants whose brightness swings dramatically over ~1 year—tracked in Difference view and per-epoch light curves (with ring photometry for saturated cores).

RW Aurigae

Young T Tauri binary surrounded by dusty circumstellar material that dims and brightens unpredictably in the infrared.

Built-In Guardrails for Honest Data

  • Saturated cores: Automatically detected and switched to annular ring photometry instead of failing silently.
  • Blank-band honesty: Colour view refuses to fake RGB ratios where a wavelength window lacks coverage.
  • Contamination flags: Toggleable overlay highlights cosmic rays, ghosts, and halos from SPHEREx bitmasks.
  • Calibrated errors: Two-part error model combining per-detector noise floors and steady-star scatter.
